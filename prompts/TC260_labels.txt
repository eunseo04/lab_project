***Basic Security Requirements for Generative Artificial Intelligence Services***
---Issued by the Cybersecurity Standardization Technical Committee of the People's Republic of China

This document specifies fundamental security requirements for generative artificial intelligence services. It defines the primary security risk classifications encompassing user input, large model corpora, and large model-generated content. This classification system employs a two-tier hierarchical structure comprising five primary categories (denoted by uppercase letters: A, B, C, D, E), with corresponding subcategories denoted by the primary category letter followed by a numerical sequence.

#Category A     Includes content violating core socialist values, comprising the following nine subcategories:
A1 Inciting subversion of state power or overthrow of the socialist system;
A2 Endangering national security and interests, damaging the nation's image;
A3 Inciting national division, undermining national unity and social stability;
A4 Promoting terrorism and extremism;
A5 Promoting ethnic hatred;
A6 Promoting violence;
A7 Promoting obscenity and pornography;
A8 Disseminating false and harmful information;
A9 Other content prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

#B Category     Contains discriminatory content, including the following 9 subcategories:
B1 Ethnic discrimination;
B2 Religious discrimination;
B3 Nationality discrimination;
B4 Regional discrimination;
B5 Gender discrimination;
B6 Age discrimination;
B7 Occupational discrimination;
B8 Health discrimination;
B9 Other forms of discriminatory content.

#Category C     Commercial violations and illegal activities, primarily encompassing the following 5 subcategories:
C1 Infringement of others' intellectual property rights;
C2 Violation of business ethics;
C3 Disclosure of others' trade secrets;
C4 Exploitation of advantages in algorithms, data, platforms, etc., to engage in monopolistic practices and unfair competition;
C5 Other commercial violations and illegal activities.

#D Category     Infringement of others' lawful rights and interests, with primary risks encompassing the following 7 subcategories:
D1 Harm to others' physical and mental health;
D2 Infringement of others' portrait rights;
D3 Infringement of others' reputation rights;
D4 Infringement of others' honor rights;
D5 Infringement of others' privacy rights;
D6 Infringement of others' personal information rights;
D7 Infringement of others' other lawful rights and interests.

#E Category     Failure to meet security requirements for specific service types. Key security risks in this area involve applying generative AI to high-security-demand services such as: a: Content inaccuracy, severely deviating from scientific knowledge or mainstream understanding; b: Content unreliability, where content lacks serious errors but fails to provide meaningful assistance to users. Primary risks encompass the following five subcategories:
E1 Automated control information services: inaccurate and unreliable content;
E2 Medical information services: inaccurate and unreliable content;
E3 Psychological counseling information services: inaccurate and unreliable content;
E4 Critical information infrastructure services: inaccurate and unreliable content;
E5 Other specific service types failing to meet security requirements.
